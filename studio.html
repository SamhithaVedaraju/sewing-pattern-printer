Claude’s detail

Best uses Claude’s most capable model, which knows garments and dressmaking in the most depth. It can take a minute or two. Faster answers sooner with a little less detail.

Solid outer line = cut. Dashed line = sew, 1.5 cm in from the edge (the seam allowance). Edges marked FOLD go on the fold of the fabric, and the arrow on each piece is the grainline.

Print at 100% / Actual size. The first page explains how to tape the sheets together, the last pages show how to sew it, and the pattern has a 5 × 5 cm square to check the scale.
