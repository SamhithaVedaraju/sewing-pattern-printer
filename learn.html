How to measure

    Size chart

    Body measurements in centimetres. The pattern adds ease on top of these, so choose by your body, not by clothes you own. If you fall between sizes, the studio’s Find my size can use your own measurements instead.

    Printing and taping

    1. Print the PDF at 100% / Actual size. “Fit to page” shrinks the pattern.
    2. Measure the 5 × 5 cm square on the pattern. If it isn’t exactly 5 cm, check your printer settings and print again.
    3. On every sheet, cut off the left and top margins along the solid scissor line. Keep the shaded tabs on the right and bottom.
    4. Start at the top-left sheet. Lay the next sheet’s trimmed edge over the shaded tab, match the lines and tape. The map on the “Start here” page shows the order.
    5. Cut each piece on the solid outer line. The dashed line inside it is where you sew, 1.5 cm in: that strip is the seam allowance.
