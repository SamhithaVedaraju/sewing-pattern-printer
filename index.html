How it works

Four steps from photo to pattern

  1. Upload a photo

    Any clear picture of the garment works. Drag a box around it so the outline can be traced.

  2. Claude reads the garment

    It names each piece of clothing, its sleeves, neckline, length and fit, and notes details like pockets or ruffles.

  3. Choose your size

    Pick XS–XL, or enter your bust, waist, hip and height and the pattern blends between sizes to fit you.

  4. Print, tape and sew

    Download a PDF for your printer, tape the sheets together, cut out the pieces and follow the steps.

What you get

Everything a first-time sewist needs

Every pattern comes with the things a shop-bought pattern puts on its envelope and instruction sheet, worked out for your garment and your size.

  • Pattern sheets for your printer

    Full-size pieces with cut and sewing lines, fold and grainline marks, tiled onto A4, Letter or any of 14 paper sizes, with tabs that show how the sheets join.

  • Instructions written for your garment

    Claude writes new step-by-step instructions and simple diagrams for every photo, in plain words. Without AI you still get clear standard steps for each style.

  • Fabric amounts and a cutting layout

    See exactly how the pieces sit on 110 cm and 150 cm fabric, and how much to buy, rounded up to the next 10 cm.

  • A supplies list

    Thread, zipper length, elastic cut to your waist, interfacing and binding, plus fabrics that are kind to beginners.

  • Help with measuring and sizing

    A diagram shows where to measure. If your bust, waist and hip fall in different sizes, the pattern grades between them.

  • Sewing words explained

    Tap words like seam allowance, selvedge or facing anywhere in the app to see what they mean.

Printing at home

Print at 100%, check the square, tape

  1. Print the PDF at 100% / Actual size, never “fit to page”.
  2. Measure the 5 × 5 cm square on the pattern. If it isn’t 5 cm, check your printer settings.
  3. Trim the left and top margins of each sheet, overlap the shaded tab and tape. The map on the first page shows the order.
  4. Cut on the solid line and sew on the dashed line.
